## The use of elliptic curve cryptography (ECC)
It may seem a bit obscure but we use the elliptical cryptography more than we even realize. So where can we find it?
- messaging apps and virtual private networks (VPNs)
- encryption within mobile phones and IoT devices
- web browsers via HTTPS
- cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum
- software and hardware authentification
Nowadays ECC is one of the most popular and secure ways to encrypt information.
